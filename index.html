    Recommendation

    Approve as requested, $1,260,000 at 70% LTV, subject to the conditions at right. The borrower's numbers understate vacancy and leave out management and reserves. Put back, the file still covers at 1.37×, above the 1.25× policy minimum, on a 10.7% debt yield.

    The thin part is rate, not rent: at +100 bp the adjusted coverage falls to 1.24×, at the covenant line. The cushion in this file is the rate lock.

    Conditions

    1. Minimum DSCR of 1.25× on adjusted NOI, tested annually from year-end statements.
    2. Replacement reserve of $300 per unit per year, escrowed with the lender.
    3. Tax reassessment confirmed with the municipal assessor before closing; rate locked at commitment.
